# Curriculum

Target learner: an adult in Bengaluru who speaks Hindi and/or Marathi and wants to hold everyday
conversations. Target level after all five lines: roughly CEFR A2 in speaking and listening for daily
situations, and able to read signboards.

The dialect is **spoken Bengaluru Kannada** (ಮಾಡ್ತೀನಿ, not ಮಾಡುತ್ತೇನೆ), with English loanwords where
Bengalureans actually use them.

## Lines

| Line | Stops | Cards | What it gives you |
|---|---|---|---|
| A · Purple · Survive | Namaskaara, Lifelines, Free Words I, Auto Stand, Directions, Darshini | 70 | First week: greet, rescue a conversation, ride an auto, order food |
| B · Green · Settle in | Numbers 1–10, Rupees and Tens, Santhe, Metro and Bus, Time of Day, The Week, Neighbours, Around Town | 98 | Prices, markets, transport, time, neighbours |
| C · Blue · Build sentences | Me and You, Question Words, I Do/I Will, I Did, Want/Can/Must, To/In/From, Big and Small, And/But/Because | 110 | Grammar through whole usable forms: pronouns, present-future, past, negation, modals, case endings, adjectives, connectors |
| D · Pink · Live here | Family, Meals, Delivery and Phone, House and Help, At Work, Doctor, Small Talk, Weather and Roads, Colours and Shopping, Bigger Numbers, Emergencies, Bengaluru Slang | 160 | The situations of actually living in the city |
| E · Orange · Read | Vowels, ka/cha rows, Ta/ta rows, pa row + ya ra la va, sha sa ha La, Vowel Signs, Stacked Letters, Read the Boards | 92 | The script via Devanagari twins; ridden in parallel with any other line |
| Majestic | Interchange | — | 15 mixed questions from lines A–D |

Boss chats (branching dialogues) hang off: Lifelines, Auto Stand, Darshini, Santhe, Neighbours, Delivery,
House, At Work, Doctor, Small Talk, Shopping.

## How a card is taught

1. **Flashcards** for the stop (optional): script, Roman, meaning, cognate, note.
2. **Ride**: 12 cards + 2 "say it aloud". New cards get recognition questions; cards you know get
   production: build-the-sentence tiles, listening, and from memory box 2 onward, **typing**.
   A miss costs ₹10 and the card returns once in a different form.
3. **Drill**: Leitner boxes at 1, 2, 4, 8, 16 days.
4. **Use**: boss chat, Sentence Forge, and a real-world field mission each day.

## Known gaps (good next contributions)

- **Listening at speed.** Audio is per phrase. There are no longer dialogues at natural speed, and no
  "which of these did you hear" minimal-pair drills (ಲ/ಳ, ನ/ಣ, short/long e and o).
- **Speaking is self-reported.** No speech recognition; the app trusts "I said it".
- **Grammar still missing:** continuous past (ಮಾಡ್ತಾ ಇದ್ದೆ), conditionals (-ದ್ರೆ), relative participles
  (ಮಾಡಿದ, ಮಾಡೋ), comparatives (-ಗಿಂತ), plurals and the genitive in full, casual ನೀನು verb forms,
  reported speech (ಅಂತ).
- **Topics missing:** bank/government office, school and children, travel outside the city, festivals,
  phone/internet trouble, cooking verbs, body parts, months and dates, clock times with halves and quarters.
- **Script:** rare letters (ಋ ಙ ಞ ಱ), numerals (೧೨೩), handwriting, reading whole sentences.
- **Native review.** Content was written carefully and machine-checked for script/Roman consistency,
  but it has not yet been proofread end to end by a native Kannada teacher. Corrections welcome.
- **Other mother tongues.** Notes assume Hindi/Marathi. A Tamil/Telugu/Malayalam track would use
  different cognates and different warnings.
